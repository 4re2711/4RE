# 词间 Wordroom

Audience: Chinese learners preparing for postgraduate English and CET exams.
Primary job: look up a word and study its meanings, usage, and distinctions in context.

Palette: paper white #ffffff, mist #f3f6f5, forest #214b3e, ink #253b34,
muted sage #64776f, pale leaf #e5edc6. Color identifies actions and learning notes.
Typography: Microsoft YaHei/Segoe UI for interface and Chinese explanations;
Georgia for English headwords and example sentences. Display 64px, headings 24px,
body 15-17px with 1.7-1.9 line height. Headwords are the visual focal point.

Layout: a quiet persistent library rail, a full-width search field, then a large
reading column with a smaller study sidebar. On mobile the rail becomes a compact
header and the reading column becomes one column. Content is left aligned.

    library rail | breadcrumb                     local status
                 | introduction                   reading note
                 | search / suggested words
                 | headword / pronunciation       notebook
                 | meanings / usage / compare     related entries
                 | active learning section

Brief review: avoid decorative dashboards and fabricated exam-frequency badges.
Use actual dictionary coverage, meaningful section counts, and real saved words.
No ornamental charts, stat-card hero, or animation without an interaction.
Only the word itself uses display type. Notes have a source label; missing data
has an honest explanation. Scoring and answer feedback must come from actual data.
